# Architecture Review Skill

## Purpose
Review proposed or implemented architecture against approved requirements, ADRs, architecture rules, quality attributes, and migration constraints.

## Checks
- Identify applicable requirements and accepted ADRs.
- Validate boundaries and dependency direction.
- Check that proposed complexity solves an evidenced requirement.
- Evaluate security, testability, observability, operability, resilience, and migration impact where applicable.
- Identify breaking contracts and unresolved decisions.
- Distinguish proposals from implemented capabilities.
- Record evidence gaps as unverified rather than assumptions.

## Output
Produce evidence-based findings using the Tech Lead severity model and final review-state contract. Do not redesign the system unless explicitly asked for alternatives after the review.
